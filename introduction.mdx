# IAG Docs

IAG (Identity & Access Governance) is a self-hosted platform that ingests
identities and their access from your directories, normalizes them into an
entitlement catalog, and runs certification campaigns over them — with every
decision recorded in a tamper-evident, hash-chained audit trail that stays
on your infrastructure.

## What you'll find here

- **[IAG Administrator Guide](/iag-admin)** — the complete, screenshot-rich
  walkthrough of every admin view: identity ingestion, entitlement catalogs,
  certification campaigns, segregation of duties, remediation and enforcement
  write-back, SCIM provisioning, risk scoring, user administration, and the
  audit chain — plus connector setup, runbooks, and troubleshooting.

## About the evidence in these docs

Every screenshot in this documentation is captured from a running build of
IAG by an automated pipeline, and pages are validated before they publish.
What you see here reflects the product as it actually is.
